# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome different experience levels
and perspectives. Critique code and evidence rather than people. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, unwanted contact and disclosure of private information
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation.
Apply these standards consistently and explain moderation decisions when possible.
For private conduct concerns, use the contact options the maintainer lists on
GitHub; do not post personal details in public issues.
